## Product Details

A laptop GPU is hardly "cloud compute", but it is genuinely "batch". Jobs queue on a local ComfyUI instance and one prompt fans out into dozens of seeds in a single run — 250 posters at 1024x1536 took 4h13m with zero failures. Because the whole chain is scripted, every image ships with its seed, full prompt and render time in a manifest, so a batch you want changed later is extended on the same layout instead of redrawn from scratch.

### Key Features

- **Priced per image**: from 100 images, unit price falls with volume — no hourly or per-revision billing
- **Reproducible**: seeds and prompts ship with the files, so any single image can be re-rendered exactly or nudged on purpose
- **Layout locked, content swapped**: the reference-image workflow keeps headline, letter spacing and footer placement while replacing the subject and palette
- **Two pipelines**: photorealism and typography on Qwen-Image-2.1, anime illustration on Anima

### Where It Works

- Storefront hero shots, detail-page imagery and food product photos
- Event posters, menus, book and playlist covers
- Concept art, storyboard pre-viz and pitch decks
- Bulk article headers and thumbnails for content sites

### Delivery & Support

- A preview sheet every 100 images before the full run ships
- If a composition misses, the prompt is revised and rerun inside the same batch — billed only for images actually produced
- Licensing: the Qwen-Image pipeline is cleared for commercial use; Anima is **non-commercial licensed**, so commercial orders always run on the Qwen pipeline
- About 1 working day per 1,000 images, with staged delivery for rush work
