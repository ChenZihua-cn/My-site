## Product Details

Takoyaki comes from Osaka. Seasoned batter is poured into the hemispherical wells of a steel plate, a piece of octopus goes in, and the balls are turned continuously with skewers until the shell is golden and crisp while the centre stays a soft, hot, semi-liquid core. We serve it exactly like a street stall: six to a box, brushed with sweet dark sauce, piped with Japanese mayonnaise, finished with dancing bonito flakes, green aonori and slivers of pickled ginger.

### Key Features

- **Crisp outside, molten inside**: the shell sets at high heat, the core stays half-liquid
- **Generous octopus**: every piece holds a pre-cooked chunk, with a clear savoury finish
- **Layered flavour**: rich sweet sauce, lightly acidic mayonnaise, smoky bonito and seaweed umami in sequence
- **Served straight off the plate**: held at plate temperature so it never arrives cold, collapsed or tough
